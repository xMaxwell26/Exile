## Expansion art and geometry

`Notorious/koios-cache/arpg-art/expansion-equipment-v1.png` is an eight-cell original expansion atlas generated with the built-in image tool, preserving the original approved equipment/currency atlases. Exact prompt and cell order: `expansion-equipment-v1.prompt.md` beside the PNG. `ExpansionIcons` keeps a 128px source for HiDPI display and rotates the six weapon illustrations into readable inventory diagonals.

Higher bases now have six distinct authored weapon meshes, two fitted armour variants, and nine original currency/gem/scroll ground meshes. Native contact sheets and sampled body-animation previews are generated by the registered higher-weapon, armour-variant and consumable checks. These are low-poly meshes on the existing character rig, not a replacement rendering engine or a complete bespoke character art set.

The skills/loadout interface is unchanged. Custom equipment, currencies, maps and gems use illustrated inventory art. The four melee weapons now use authored low-poly geometry for ground and equipped rendering. Armour and utility item meshes still use tinted cache geometry.

Runtime art assets:

The client reads atlas cells and downsamples at runtime into its 32px inventory slots, including selection outlines. Previous procedural art remains a fallback if an atlas is missing.

Equipment uses dedicated IDs 22420–22426; map/skill gem/support gem use 22410–22412. Ordinary RuneScape equipment is unchanged. Saved rolled prototype items migrate by their roll metadata, preserving UUID, affixes, corruption, identification, gem/map data and quantities. Old rune currency placeholders can be exchanged through P / Guide.

Run `CustomItemVisualCheck` to verify matching client/server definitions, native icons, and actual cache models for ground and both worn genders. It creates `Notorious/Server/build/custom-items.png`. `ItemInventoryArtCheck` creates a 32px inventory-scale preview.

## Authored weapon meshes

`CustomWeaponModels.java` builds four original meshes: a chipped Ashbound blade with cyan inlay, a hooked Duskfang blade, an eight-flange Gravebell mace with ember core, and a crescent Riftcleaver axe with red inlay. These replace the cached weapon geometry; they are not just recolors. The approved inventory PNGs and loadout interface remain unchanged.

For worn items, the template supplies only the grip anchor and weapon-hand animation group (50). Every authored vertex binds to that group. Separate cache material styling continues for armour. Dropped weapons use the same authored shape with a ground-space orientation. Polygon triangulation, integer vertex cleanup, native lighting/rendering and skeletal group transforms are covered by WeaponMeshCheck.

`dev.ps1 Test` includes cache/atlas checks and authored-mesh checks. The native renderer preview is `Notorious/Server/build/weapon-meshes.png`. Live movement, attack animation and body clipping still need an in-game check. Original armour and currency/gem meshes remain later work.
